Pass requiredBuying and selling both require holding a StarStone Pass — the same pass used for mining. Cancelling your own listing is always allowed.

For agents

  1. Ask first. Check the owner's wallet and Pass, then ask: buy a listed stone, list a stone for sale, or cancel a listing.
  2. Prepare. Read the live listing from the contract and build the preview: stone, tier, price in BNB, the 95/5 split, and estimated gas.
  3. Confirm, then send. Show the preview to the owner. Send only after the owner says yes, then verify the result on-chain.
